# M1 Walmart P1 conditional forecast and valuation review

The [Core linked forecast](../02_financial_core/conditional_linked_forecast.json) starts from Walmart's disclosed April 30, 2026 consolidated balance sheet. It links the remaining FY27 period through FY31 income statements, balance sheets, cash flows, FCFF, and FCFE. The [M1 valuation screen](conditional_valuation_screen.json) consumes the Core FCFF path and shows a nominal USD WACC, terminal economics, a dated DCF calculation, and a partial enterprise-to-equity bridge. This is an analyst scenario constructed retrospectively on October 1, 2026. It was not a forecast frozen on the May 21 information date or a conversion of company constant-currency guidance into reported GAAP dollars.

The arithmetic screen is not eligible for publication as a company value. Market observations lack fully verified as-of publication times or usage rights. The lease treatment, allocation of depreciation and amortization, May 1-20 cash flows before the valuation date, sustainable terminal ROIC, and May 21 claims remain unresolved. The terminal artifact also exposes an unsupported step of about 1.77 times from final Core FCFF to next-year terminal FCFF. The partial bridge preserves unknown excess cash, nonoperating assets, and other senior claims as `UNKNOWN`, with `equity_value=None`. M1 and the release remain `WITHHELD`.

The generated [memo fields](conditional_memo_fields.json) point to Core, DCF, and partial bridge output IDs without copying a numeric valuation into the memo. Read the [method screen](method_screen.md), [decision memo](memo.md), [business economics](business_economics.md), [history window](history_window.md), [gate results](../07_validation_governance/gate_results.csv), and [handover](../07_validation_governance/handover.md) together. Rows in `forecast_versions.csv` and `valuation_runs.csv` describe a review run, not a published enterprise value or investment opinion.

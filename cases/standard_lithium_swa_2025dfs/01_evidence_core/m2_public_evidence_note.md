# SWA M2 public evidence additions

This note records only facts that were public by the 2025-10-15 ET cutoff and
the boundaries that stop them becoming an M2 valuation.

- The SEC-filed DFS says SWA Lithium LLC pays TETRA a USD 1 million annual
  lease-maintenance fee before commercial operations.  It is a real disclosed
  status-quo cash item, but no due-date sequence, abandonment path, grant
  consequence, or rights-expiry cash path is supplied.
- The SEC-filed 2024 Annual Information Form says Equinor solely funds the
  first USD 40 million of SWA development costs, after which capital spending
  is pro rata.  It also describes a USD 40 million FID milestone payment to
  Standard Lithium subject to FID dates.  Neither statement says what remains
  unspent, called, funded, or payable at the cutoff.
- A SEC-filed 2025-01-16 release says SWA Lithium LLC closed a USD 225 million
  DOE grant supporting Phase 1.  It is not treated as cash on hand or as an
  unconditional source because draw, compliance, cost-share, and allocation
  terms are not disclosed in that release.

`04_m2_capital_allocation/public_m2_evidence.csv` preserves the receipt
lineage and feeds the reproducible completeness output.  It deliberately
leaves the decision `WITHHELD`; these facts cannot date the DFS's relative
periods, separate sunk from remaining capex, or supply a complete no-build
path.

# M3 case selection under P4 Gates A–H

**Selected mode: C — evidence-gap memo.** This finance-company case tests whether the system can keep a consolidated liquidity disclosure apart from a legal obligor's cash and keep securitization debt separate from parent unsecured debt. It is not a full credit pack.

| Gate | Evidence at the 2026-02-11 cutoff | State |
|---|---|---|
| A — public rights | Official SEC filing can be accessed and cited; raw HTML redistribution is unconfirmed, so only locator/hash and selected transformed cells are retained. | Locator-only |
| B — as-of | SEC filing and acceptance dates known; conservative end-of-filing-date publication bound recorded. | Pass with timestamp precision limit |
| C — obligor | Filer, selected subsidiary borrowers and securitization-entity distinction identified; aggregate unsecured debt cannot all be assigned to one legal issuer. | Partial |
| D — cash | 2025 consolidated cash identity and company-defined group liquidity bridge reconcile; parent-only accessible cash does not. | Partial |
| E — debt | 2025-12-31 principal buckets and total carrying adjustment reconcile; exact dates, instrument-level face/carrying and committed refinancing do not. | Partial |
| F — contract | Facility commitment and some covenant types disclosed; drawability and headroom untested, recovery value pool absent. | Withheld |
| G — stress | Public product/asset risk indicators exist, but no dated legal-obligor cash path or supported first failure trigger. | Withheld |
| H — outcome | A later Q2 filing is separately stored, but no released ex-ante forecast exists to score. | Not yet testable |

The case was chosen over reusing the Orion manufacturing company because Ford Credit's official 10-K supplies a clear securitization constraint and a same-vintage cash, debt, maturity and liquidity disclosure set. That extra data strengthens the evidence-boundary test; it does not cure the missing legal-entity cash path.
